KANJIDIC2

Japanese kun-reading okurigana boundaries in unihan_cjkv.bin are derived
and adapted from KANJIDIC2, maintained by the Electronic Dictionary
Research and Development Group (EDRDG).

Project and source data:
https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/KANJIDIC_Project.html
https://www.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/kanjidic2.xml.gz

Changes made for FusionIME:
- selected Japanese kun readings only
- converted KANJIDIC2 okurigana separators from "." to "-"
- merged the marked readings with selected Unicode Unihan properties
- encoded the result in FusionIME's compact binary dictionary format
